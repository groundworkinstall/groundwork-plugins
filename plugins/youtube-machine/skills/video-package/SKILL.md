# Video package

Read the youtube-machine skill's `references/rules.md`, the tracker row, the brief and the trimmed transcript (in the video's folder) first. Get the time from the app's time tool before you write any date. Work from the transcript, so every chapter time and every quote is real.

Set Step `7 Video package`, Status `In progress`.

Write a Google Doc `Package: [working title]` in the video's folder, each part under its own heading so it can be copied one at a time:

1. **Title, three choices.** Each under 60 characters, using the words people search with (from the brief's part 1). No clickbait, no all caps. Mark your pick and say why in one line.
2. **Description.** First two lines say what the video answers and who it's for (these show before "more"). Then three to five short lines on what's covered. Then the checklist line with its signup link (the Settings tab's `Signup link`; `[signup link]` if not made yet). Then how to reach the business. Then the planning-not-advice line from `rules.md`.
3. **Chapters.** `0:00 Intro` first, then one line per section with its time from the transcript, at least three, each at least 10 seconds apart. These go at the bottom of the description.
4. **Tags.** Eight to twelve, from the brief's search words.
5. **Thumbnail words.** Three choices, each four words or fewer, plus one line on the layout: who's in the shot, where the words sit, one color. Canva's free plan is enough; a designer is fine too.
6. **Pinned comment.** One or two lines with the checklist offer and link.
7. **Short clips.** For each clip picked in Descript: its start and end time, a title under 40 characters, and a caption of two lines or fewer. The same clip and caption go to YouTube Shorts and the business's other social accounts, cut once and posted the same everywhere.
8. **Social post.** A short post for the business's main social page (or its regular live session, if it has one) announcing the video, in their voice.

Then ask them to pick: one title, one set of thumbnail words. Write the picks at the top of the doc.

**Check before Done:** read the package back; all eight parts are there; the chapter times exist in the transcript; the picks are at the top. Put the link in `Package`, add a Log row, and go to step 8, the email draft.
