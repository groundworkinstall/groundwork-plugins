# Research brief

Read the youtube-machine skill's `references/rules.md` and the tracker's Settings tab first. Get the time from the app's time tool before you write any date.

## The brief, one page

Set the video's Step to `2 Research brief`, Status `In progress`, before you search.

Research the topic with web search. Use the most original sources you can find (government pages, the organization that sets a rule, the maker of a product, recent news from named outlets). Read the page, not just the search snippet. Then write a Google Doc titled `Brief: [working title]` in the video's folder, with these five parts:

1. **What people are asking.** Five to eight questions people ask about this topic, in their own words where you found them (search results, question sites, video comments). Then one line on what the top two or three videos on this topic say, and what they leave out.
2. **The facts that matter.** Each fact on its own line: `[fact] (source: [name], [link], checked [date])`. Costs, rules, deadlines, program names. If two sources disagree, give both and say which you'd use and why.
3. **What changed lately.** New rules, news or policy changes in the last 12 months, each dated. If nothing changed, write "Nothing found in the last 12 months" and the searches you ran.
4. **Watch-outs.** Anything the video could get wrong or that would cross into medical, legal, tax or financial advice, each with the line to say instead.
5. **Interview questions.** Five to seven, one at a time, in plain words, built from parts 1 to 4. The first asks what people usually get wrong; the last asks for the one thing a viewer should do this week.

At the top, one line: `Brief written [date]. [N] sources.`

Before you use a fact, check the What we know doc. If the same fact is there checked within 12 months, reuse it. Then add every new or rechecked fact to What we know, one line each.

Unless the Settings tab says `Run type: Test`, ask once before you write part 1: "Any questions from your own customers, your social media comments or your events on this topic? Paste or say them. I can't read Facebook, TikTok, Instagram or Reddit myself." Use what they give you first; it's often the best material.

**Check before Done:** read the brief back. Every fact line has a source and a date. All five parts are there. Then put the link in the tracker's `Brief` column with the time, add a Log row, and tell them in two lines what's in it and the next step (the interview). Reading the brief takes them about five minutes; offer it, don't require it.

## Monthly field scan

Run when the youtube-machine skill says it's due, or when asked.

1. Search the business's field for the last month: news, rule and program changes, new questions people are asking. Two to five searches per main subject area on the Settings tab.
2. Add a dated section to the top of a Google Doc titled `Field scan` in the machine's folder (make it once; then add to it in place): `[Month year]`, then what you found, each with source and date.
3. Add any new topic ideas to the Topics tab as `Open`, with a note saying they came from the field scan.
4. Add anything worth one line in the next monthly email under a heading `For the monthly email` in that section.
5. Set `Last field scan` on the Settings tab to today, add a `Field scan` Log row, and tell them in three lines or fewer what you found.
