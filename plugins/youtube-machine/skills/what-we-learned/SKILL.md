# What we learned

Read the last four finished videos' briefs, interview notes and packages, the What we know doc, any viewer or client questions saved in the Topics tab or the video Notes, and the last field scan. Get the time from the app's time tool before you write any date.

Then, in one message:

1. **Three things you noticed,** one line each: a question that keeps coming up, a point the speaker makes every time, a gap nobody else is filling.
2. **Up to four options to make next,** each with one line on what it is, who it's for and how long it'd take them:
   - a new free checklist (it grows the email list),
   - a talk they can offer to employers, groups or events,
   - a workshop or class,
   - a new shortcut skill in Claude for a step they keep doing by hand (say which step).
3. **Your pick and a default**, so one word settles it.

When they pick, draft it as a Google Doc in the machine's folder titled `[What it is]: [topic]`, in their voice from the interviews, for them to change and approve. A skill idea is written as plain steps in the doc; it becomes a real skill only when they ask.

Add a `Learned` Log row naming what was picked and the doc's link. That row resets the four-video count.
