# Business Development Hub 0.1.0

Keeps track of the people who can bring you work: clients, leads, and the people who send them to you. It runs on its own; no other plugin is needed.

**Start it:** type "Set up my hub" in a new chat. It takes about 90 minutes.

**What it makes:** a Business Development project, a one-page business development plan with five people to ask this week and a draft of each ask, your contacts in one place, a morning page rewritten each weekday at 7:00am, and a private Hub page for your computer and phone. Claude keeps its notes in Notion so it remembers your work from one chat to the next.

**Skills:**
- hub: the Hub session, saving what happened after each conversation, a business card photo, check my email, Friday review, back up my contacts, rewrite my Hub Today, writing style, look, park it, get help.
- import-contacts: brings in a contact list or export, with a preview and nothing written before a yes, and fill in my contacts, 10 at a time.
- prep: a brief in seven sections before a meeting, saved on that person in your Hub.
- research: research this person, one pass, saved on their page.

**What it never does:** send, post, book, pay or delete anything; change your settings; or touch Notion pages it didn't make (beyond one line on another kit plugin's home page).

**Installing it:** upload business-development-hub.plugin in Claude's settings, under Plugins. Then close that screen; you don't need Publish or Share.

**Notes for the kit maker:** every word the owner sees is in skills/hub/references/messages.md, generated from the Google Doc "What the client sees: Business Development Hub". Change the words in the Doc and rebuild. The files basics.md, rules.md, writing-style.md, plain-words.md, look.md, look-rules.md, get-help.md and park-it.md in skills/hub/references are the kit's shared pieces, kept byte for byte the same in every kit plugin. Version is recorded in the Canonical Files row.
