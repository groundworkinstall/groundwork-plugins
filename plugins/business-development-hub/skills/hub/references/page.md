# The Hub page, button by button

From the script, word for word. Every write the page makes is listed here; anything not listed, it doesn't do. Use it to answer questions about the page and to publish it.

The page is hub.html, published private in the owner's account. It reads Basics (name, time zone, brand), Hub Settings and Hub Plan, and Contacts and the Log through their view links. Writes go to the owner's Notion through the page's Notion connector. Nothing on the page sends email, texts or invites. Every write the page makes is listed here; anything not listed, it doesn't do. The page also keeps a little of its own: each tap (Done, Happened, Copy, Sent, Later, Skip, Go, Hold) and whether Notion took it, in the page's own small store, and on the device the last 10 notes from Tell the hub and whether each one saved, whether help is shown, whether the first-open tour has been seen, and the tab last open. None of that goes anywhere else.
### Top and bottom of the page
- Title: the business name plus Hub ("Lopez Home Care Hub"), from Basics. Beside it, the time line "Updated Thu Oct 22, 7:02am": the time of the newest "Hub Today rewritten" Log row, in the owner's time zone. Read only.
- Tabs and counts: Today "5 for you" (today's cards on Ready for you, not next-weekday meeting cards, and the Go or hold cards, each counted while not yet tapped today); People "129" (everyone in Contacts not Archived); Outreach "3" (this week's cards not yet handled with Sent, Later or Skip); Briefs "2" (briefs for meetings today or later). A count of 0 shows as 0. Read only.
- The question mark: shows or hides the one-line help notes (see Help).
- Footer, at the bottom of every tab, one line: "Saved" when every tap and note has gone through ("Saving" while one is going, "Not saved yet" when one hasn't, with Try again on that card or note); "Your Hub read 8:40am", the time the page last read Contacts; "Built with Groundwork"; and "Show the tour again", which opens the first-open tour at card 1. The footer never says Notion, Log or tracker. Nothing in the footer writes to Notion.
### Top of every tab: Tell the hub
The owner types or dictates what happened and taps Save. What happens: the page matches names in the text to Contacts (email, full name, or a first name with its company; a first name alone isn't checked, so the owner ticks it) and shows who it will go to, with checkboxes, a search box to add someone, and an unchecked "Add Rosa Delgado as a new contact" when no one has that full name (Rosa Delgado stands for the name in the note). Save to people: for each person, one dated line under History ending "(Hub page)" and Last talked set to today; a new contact is made with Name, First name and Last name only. Then one Log row (Status, "Hub page note"). Save with nobody picked: one Log row (Open item) for the Hub chat to sort. Back: nothing is saved. After Save, one line under the box, for one person: "Saved to «Tom Becker»'s notes. His next step and stage update the next time you open a Business Development chat, or by tomorrow morning." (her or their in place of his, from the person's first name only when it's clear; their when it isn't; for two or more people, "Saved to Tom Becker's and Gina Moss's notes. Their next steps and stages update the next time you open a Business Development chat, or by tomorrow morning."). From Friday after 7:00am through Sunday, each line ends "or by Monday morning." in place of "or by tomorrow morning." With nobody picked: "Saved. Your next Business Development chat will ask you who it's for." Right after Save the page reads Contacts again, so a new contact's card shows on People straight away with just their name and "New today". The next Business Development chat gives a contact saved with only a name its About and fills its blanks; their next step and stage come from the next chat or the next morning task. The page never changes Next step or Stage; the next Business Development chat does that from the History line, and so does the 7:00am morning task for any Hub page note a chat hasn't worked yet. Your notes: a fold-out under the box, "Your notes" ("Your notes, 1 not saved" when one hasn't gone through), with the last 10 notes on this device, newest first. Each shows the note and either "Saved to Tom Becker Thu 9:20am" or "Not saved yet" with Try again. With no notes yet: "Nothing here yet. Notes you save show here." The list lives on the device only; nothing in it is sent anywhere else, and clearing it changes nothing in the Hub. If Notion doesn't take it, the note stays in the box and in Your notes as "Not saved yet" with Try again. The page's error lines never name Notion: "reconnect your notes app in Settings, Connectors", "your notes app isn't connected on this account", "your notes app said:" and its message, and "your notes app isn't reachable right now".
### Today tab
- Top line, always shown: "What needs you today, and anything waiting on a go or hold." The tab has three groups only, in this order: From the hub chat today, Ready for you, Go or hold. Nothing else shows on Today.
- From the hub chat today: up to 6 of today's Log rows written by a Business Development chat, newest first, each a time and a plain line ("8:31am Tom Becker is sending two families your way and wants your one-pager"), with the type label ("Status:") taken off. Rows the page wrote itself, "Hub page note worked:" rows and the morning task's "Hub Today rewritten" are left out. Hidden when a chat hasn't saved anything today. Read only.
- Ready for you: one card per thing that needs the owner today, each with the name, company and a pill. Three kinds: (1) a next step: each person whose Next step date is today or earlier, with their next step and its day. Someone the owner is waiting on shows here only on their check-in day, the Next step date, with a "check in today" pill when the next step starts "Waiting on"; before that day they're not on Today. Anyone on this week's Outreach (Outreach week is this Monday) with no Ask sent, Ask moved to later or Ask skipped this week Log row this week has no next-step card here; they're handled on Outreach. (2) A meeting today or on the next weekday (on Friday, Monday's) with someone in Contacts, matched by email or name and read live from the calendar, with the day and time as the pill, the event's title and place, and "Their brief is ready." when they have a brief for it. (3) A follow-up draft the owner said yes to in a chat: anyone whose Follow-up draft isn't empty, with a "draft ready" pill, one line on what it's for and the draft. One person, one card: a meeting card takes in that person's next-step card for the same day. Buttons: next steps get Done, Later and Note; meetings today get Happened and Note, and next-weekday meetings get Note only (Happened shows on the meeting day); drafts get Copy, Sent and Later. After a tap the card dims and folds to one line with the time, like "Done 9:14am." or "Later, back tomorrow.", and Undo.
- Calendar lines, shown under Ready for you as information, never as errors: "Meeting cards don't work with Outlook here yet. Your meetings are in your 7:00am summary, under Scheduled."; "Connect Microsoft 365 in Settings, Connectors to see your meetings here."; "Calendar access wasn't allowed for this page. Reopen it and allow it when asked."; "Microsoft 365 isn't reachable right now. It tries again on its own."
- Done: the card stays on Ready for you for today, folded and marked done; it comes back tomorrow until the chat sets a new Next step date. Last confirmed set to today, a History line "Done: «next step» (Hub page)", one Log row (Status). Next step is not changed; the owner tells the chat what's next. Undo: removes the tap and writes a Correction line and a Correction Log row; nothing is deleted and Last confirmed isn't rolled back.
- Note: puts the person's name in Tell the hub, so the owner can add what happened. Nothing is saved until Save.
- Later (on a next step or a draft card): one Log row (Status, "Moved to tomorrow:" and the name). The card folds with "Later, back tomorrow." and comes back on tomorrow's Today. The contact isn't changed.
- Happened (on a meeting card, on the meeting day): a History line "Met: Oct 22 (Hub page)", Last talked set to today, and one Log row (Status, "Met:" and the name). The card folds with "Happened 2:50pm." The owner tells a chat how it went; the next step comes from that. Undo works like Done's. There's no Moved button: the card reads the calendar live, so a moved meeting moves itself.
- Copy (on a draft card): copies the draft to the clipboard. The card shows "Copied, not sent yet". Nothing is written and nothing is counted.
- Sent (on a draft card): for after the owner has sent it themselves. A History line "Follow-up sent (Hub page)", Last talked set to today, and one Log row (Status, "Follow-up sent:" and the name). It isn't counted as an ask. The card folds with "Sent 9:14am." and the next chat clears Follow-up draft. Undo works like Done's.
- Go or hold: one card per person whose Go or hold field isn't empty, after Ready for you. Each card has the name (and through whom, when someone's introducing them), company, what Go means and what Hold means, a line "Default go:" or "Default hold:" with Why the default, and Go and Hold buttons with the default marked "· default". Go or Hold: a History line "Go: what it was (Hub page)" or "Hold: what it was (Hub page)" and one Log row (Status, "Go:" or "Hold:" and the name). The card folds with "Go 9:14am." or "Hold 9:14am." and Undo; the next chat acts on it and clears the card. Hidden when there are none.
### People tab
- Top line, always shown: "Everyone in your Hub. Search anything, and tap a card for their notes and history."
- Search: the box reads "Search a name, a company, a referrer". It finds people whose row has every word typed, anywhere in it: name, first name, last name, Also known as (so a name inside a group row), company, title, email, second email, Who they are, Stage, Next step, About, Referred by, ignoring capitals and accents. No match: "No match. Tell the hub the name and a card gets added." Read only.
- Filter chips: All, Waiting on you, Referrers, Prospects, Quiet 30+ days. Waiting on you is anyone with a card on Today's Ready for you or Go or hold. Referrers is Who they are Sends me work or Partner. Prospects is Who they are Lead. Quiet 30+ days is anyone whose Last talked is 30 or more days ago. One chip at a time; All is on when the page opens. Read only.
- A person's card, closed: name, a Brief button when they have a brief, their Stage where the demo had dots, company and title, then "Next" and their next step with its day. Anyone added today shows "New today" under their name. Tap to open.
- A person's card, open: who they are in small capitals, the About line, then Email, Phone, Website, LinkedIn ("His profile", "Her profile" or "Their profile", a link, chosen the same way as the after-Save line), Address and "Last talked Oct 8", each only when it's filled in, then History, newest first, or "No history written yet." On a computer the open card spans the full width. Read only.
- Brief: opens the Briefs tab on that person's brief. Nothing is written.
- Order: people waiting on you first (the Waiting on you chip), then the rest by Stage, in the order the owner's stages are set in Hub Settings (New, Talking, Proposal out, Client, Not now to start), and within each, most recent Last talked first.
### Outreach tab
- Lede, always shown at the top: "Each Monday the Hub picks this week's people from your plan and drafts every ask. You send them yourself, then tap Sent. Your plan came out of our setup session on Oct 23: who you're looking for, who sends you work, and what you're asking for." The date is the day the owner's Hub Plan was made.
- Your goals: heading "Your goals", "set when we built your plan". Bars: asks sent this week against Asks each week in Hub Plan ("Asks sent this week 2 of 5 sent"); conversations booked against Conversations each week ("Conversations booked this week 1 of 2 booked"); the owner's goal when the Hub can count it. No dollar goals. Counts come from this week's Log rows (Monday to today, owner's time zone). Read only.
- The plan: heading "The plan", "who you're reaching, how often, and what you ask for". One row per group from Plan groups in Hub Plan: the group's name, how often (only when the owner gave one, like "Every 3 weeks"), who's in it, and "The ask:" with the ask. Read only.
- This week's outreach: heading with "week of Oct 19, picked Monday 7:00am" (this Monday's date, and the time of that morning's "Hub Today rewritten" Log row). Under it, "3 of 5 handled. 2 sent, 1 later or skipped, 2 to go." and a bar for the handled share, then "People with something already open are on Today, so they're left off this list." One card per person with Outreach week set to this Monday: name, title or company, a group tag (their Who they are), "Why this week:" and the line from Why this week, the kind of ask and the day (Next step date), and the two drafts, labeled Text and Email.
- Copy (one under each draft): copies that draft to the clipboard. The card shows "Copied, not sent yet". Nothing is written to Notion and nothing is counted.
- Sent: for after the owner has sent it themselves, from their own email or phone. The card folds with "Sent 9:14am. Counted toward this week." and Undo. A History line "Ask sent: «kind of ask» (Hub page)", Last talked set to today, Ask landed set to No reply yet, and one Log row (Status, "Ask sent:" and the name), which is what the goal bar and the Friday review count. Undo works like Done's; the goal bar, the 7:00am update and Friday review leave out any "Ask sent" row that has a later Correction row for the same person.
- Later: the card folds with "Later 9:14am." and counts as handled. One Log row (Status, "Ask moved to later:" and the name). The page hides any card whose person has an "Ask moved to later" or "Ask skipped this week" Log row since Monday; the next Monday's pick can choose them again. The contact isn't changed.
- Skip: the card folds with "Skipped 9:14am." and counts as handled. One Log row (Status, "Ask skipped this week:" and the name). The page hides the card the same way. The contact isn't changed.
- Asks that worked: from Hub Plan, filled by the Friday review. Read only.
### Briefs tab
- Lede, always shown at the top: "Before a meeting, Claude reads what's public about the person and their business, checks it against what's in your Hub, and writes a brief you can read in five minutes."
- Picking a brief: one button per brief with the person's name, for meetings today or later (Brief for today or later), soonest meeting first, up to 8. Below them, "Older briefs": plain lines with the name and meeting day, newest first. From the Brief, Brief for and Brief written fields in Contacts.
- Tap a name or an older brief: the whole brief opens. Header: the name; title and company ("Owner, Shah Elder Law"); the meeting, read live from the calendar ("Coffee at Stauf's, Thu Oct 22, 10:00 to 10:45am"), or the Brief for day when the event can't be found; and "Written by Claude Wed Oct 21, 7:04am." from Brief written. Then the brief in its seven sections as saved: Who they are; What they're likely dealing with; Where you could help; Questions to ask; Keep out of the first meeting; Suggested next step (in a shaded box); Sources. Read only. A new brief comes from the morning task or "prep me for my meeting with".
### Help
- The question mark at the top shows or hides the one-line help notes. Saved on the page only. The always-on line at the top of each tab stays either way. The help notes, word for word: Tell the hub: "Tell your Hub what happened. Name the people; I'll add it to their notes." Your notes: "Your last 10 notes on this phone or computer, and whether each one saved." From the hub chat today: "What your Business Development chats saved today." Ready for you: "Due today, check-ins due today, meetings today and on the next weekday, and drafts you said yes to." Done: "Tap when you've done the next step, then tell me what's next." Later, on Today: "Off today. It's back tomorrow." Happened: "Tap after the meeting, then tell me how it went." Go or hold: "A yes or no for you. The default is marked." People: "Search anything: a name, a company, a next step." Brief, on a card: "Opens their brief." Outreach: "This week's asks against your plan. Copy a draft, send it yourself, then tap Sent." Copy: "Copies the draft. Nothing is sent." Sent: "Tap after you've sent it yourself. This counts it." Sent, on a follow-up draft: "Tap after you've sent it yourself." Later, on Outreach: "Off this week's list. They can come back next Monday." Skip: "This ask doesn't fit this week. Off the list." Briefs: "Your meeting briefs, soonest meeting first."

### First open
The first time the page opens on a phone or computer, five short cards show one at a time over the page, labeled "First time you open it · 1 of 5", with dots, Back and Next. Back shows from card 2 on. The last card has Back and Done in place of Next; Done closes the tour. The cards, word for word:
1. "After a call, coffee or email, type what happened in the box at the top of any tab, in your own words, and tap Save. Name the people and it goes on their notes right away. The next time you open a chat in your Business Development project, or by the next weekday morning, Claude reads it and updates what's next with them."
2. "Today is your business development list for the day, in three parts. From the hub chat today: what you told Claude today. Ready for you: who to reach out to or follow up with, and your meetings. Go or hold: things waiting on a yes or no from you. Tap the buttons as you go. To change anything else, tell Claude in your Business Development project."
3. "People is everyone in your Hub. Search a name or a company, and tap a card to see their notes and history."
4. "Outreach is this week's asks, picked every Monday from your plan, each with two drafts: a short text and an email. Copy one, send it yourself from your own email or phone, then tap Sent so it's counted."
5. "Briefs has a short brief for each meeting with someone in your Hub, ready by 7:00am on the day and usually the morning before. In a Business Development chat you can also type: prep me for my meeting with, research this person, fill in my contacts, check my email, Friday review, or park it."
What happens: the page remembers on the device that the tour was seen, so it doesn't show again there. "Show the tour again" in the footer opens it at card 1, labeled "Tour · 1 of 5". Nothing goes to Notion.
