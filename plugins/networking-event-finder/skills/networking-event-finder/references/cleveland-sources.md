# Northeast Ohio event calendars

These pages returned real event listings in October 2026. Check each one every search. Notes say where a page is hard to read.

## Business groups and centers
- COSE small business events: https://cose.org/events/
- Greater Cleveland Partnership events (AI roundtables, forums): https://greatercle.com/events/
- GCP member calendar (Coffee with COSE and registration): https://member.greatercle.com/Events/Calendar-Of-Events
- JumpStart events (also lists GCP, Tri-C and CSU events): https://www.jumpstartinc.org/events/
- Rotary Club of Cleveland (weekly Thursday lunch, speaker schedule): https://www.rotaryclubofcleveland.com/

## Chambers of commerce (east side, Lake County, Summit County edge)
- Beachwood Chamber: https://public.beachwood.org/events/
- Heights-Hillcrest Regional Chamber: https://members.hrcc.org/events
- Solon Chamber (homepage lists upcoming events; full calendar needs a browser): https://www.solonchamber.com/
- Mayfield Area Chamber (blocks some automated reading): https://www.mayfieldareachamber.org/events/
- Chagrin Valley Chamber: https://www.cvcc.org/events/calendar
- Twinsburg Chamber: https://business.twinsburgchamber.com/events/calendar
- Mentor Area Chamber: https://business.mentorchamber.org/events/calendar
- Lake County Chamber: https://business.lakecountychamber.com/events/calendar

For the west side, search for the Westlake, Rocky River, Lakewood, Strongsville and North Olmsted chambers and the Westshore groups.

## What was hard to find in 2026
- BNI meeting times and venues load by script. Say so and give the chapter finder link.
- Many chambers say "free for members" and don't list a non-member price.
- EO Cleveland and Vistage are membership groups with no open events.
- Trade associations (dental, medical, restaurant, real estate, CPA, financial planning) rarely post open Cleveland networking events. Search anyway when the person names one of these industries.
